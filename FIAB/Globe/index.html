About, data and limitations

Forecast. The operational AIFS Single (ECMWF's Artificial Intelligence Forecasting System, deterministic), run every 6 hours on a grid of about 28 km. Shown here on a 1° grid (about 110 km) every 6 hours to +240 h; hourly frames are interpolated linearly in between. Precipitation is the 6 h total shown as a mean rate in mm per hour, drawn as snow where the 2 m temperature is below +1 °C.

Terrain is the 0.25° model orography, vertically exaggerated; the weather is on the coarser 1° grid. Wind trails show the 10 m wind over the surface.

Limitations. An experimental visualisation, not an official forecast or warning. Interpolation to 1° smooths the forecast: small-scale rain and wind extremes are weaker than in the model.
