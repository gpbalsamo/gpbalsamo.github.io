IFS Land, Rivers, Floods Benchmarking Dashboards

ecLand–CaMa-Flood (IFS) model vs. in-situ flux-tower, river-gauge and satellite flood observations

ifs-landbench

Offline single-point ecLand runs scored against flux-tower observations — latent and sensible heat, net ecosystem exchange, methane flux, and soil moisture and temperature. Dashboards are served from the ifs-landbench repository.

All 775 Sites

The full FLUXNET-Shuttle pool across AmeriFlux, ICOS and TERN: 775 towers, 1507 site-years. Median r of 0.79 (Qle) and 0.82 (Qh).

PLUMBER2 Subset

The PLUMBER2 pool re-scored over FLUXNET-Shuttle's longer records: 110 of the 170 towers, 1551 site-years against the original 800.

Curated Best-42 Subset

The 42 towers recommended for automated benchmarking, chosen for spatial and biome diversity: 36 available here, 664 site-years.

FLUXNET-CH4

Methane flux against the FLUXNET-CH4 Community Product v1.0: 79 towers of observations, 46 scored. Emission is gated by the wetland fraction.

ifs-riverbench

River discharge from the IFS river routing component, scored against gauge observations.

Discharge vs. Gauges

River discharge model experiments vs. gauge observations, by station, with a river-network diagnostic view.

ifs-floodbench

Flood extent from CaMa-Flood coupled to the IFS, scored against satellite flood mapping (VIIRS, GFM, MODIS) over three event catalogues.

KuroSiwo Flood Events

Model vs. VIIRS / GFM / MODIS observations for 43 events from the KuroSiwo benchmark catalogue.

Post-2016 Named Events

Model vs. observations for 17 hand-picked notable global floods since 2016.

DE 2026 Events

Model vs. observations for 10 recent, currently-monitored flood events.
